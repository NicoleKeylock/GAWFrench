GAW portfolio context — where we sit in this market

Provence
Premium rosé is stalling: −0.1% value, −2.4% volume. Avg bottle £11.74. The £15+ segment is up +7% but volume is thin. Brand strength and distribution are the key differentiators at this end of the market.
Rhône / VdF (LVF)
Rhône without LVF is £156.9M (+4.2%). VdF — which is entirely LVF — is £185.9M (+42.8%). LVF is the dominant force in French wine and the key competitor across rosé, white and red.
Bordeaux
Challenging: −1.9% value, −6.4% volume. The £12–15 tier is the worst-performing price band (−12% value). Own label dominates and is also declining. Positioning and distribution focus is key.
