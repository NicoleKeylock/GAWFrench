GAW portfolio context — where we sit in this market

Provence
The premium rosé heartland is stalling: −0.2% value, −2.5% volume. Avg. bottle price £11.74. The £15+ segment (Whispering Angel territory) is up +7% but volume is thin. GAW exposure here is in a softening market — price and brand strength matter more than ever.
Rhône / VdF (LVF)
Nielsen records Rhône at +21% value / +20% volume — but this is almost entirely LVF. The underlying Rhône category without LVF is much more modest. As the brand owner, GAW is the primary driver of this regional number. A significant competitive moat, but also concentration risk.
Bordeaux
A challenging picture: −1.9% value, −6.4% volume. The £12–15 tier (where much classic Bordeaux sits) is down −12% value. Private label dominates and is also declining. Any GAW Bordeaux SKUs are swimming against the tide — positioning and distribution focus is key.
